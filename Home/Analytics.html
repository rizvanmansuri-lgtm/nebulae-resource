Nebulink Analytics

Introduction

The plugin inserted in the adaptors of Middleware enables you to download plugins into your system/device. These plugins provide you with data analytics capabilities. Nebulae analytics can transform vast amounts of raw unstructured data generated from your IoT system into well-structured data.

By visualization and simulation of events and modeling new use case scenarios, data processing enhances the quality of service offered to end-users, prevents accidents, and enhances preparedness regarding disaster management.

Our Features

Data Analytics

NebuLink Analytics enables you complete freedom to process collected raw data into an analytics system of your choice. It transforms the unstructured data into a well-structured convenient for analytics, pattern analysis, visualization, and charting.

Data visualization

By employing a rich set of configurable widgets, NebuLink Analytics provides interactive, real-time, and multidimensional data visualization of both historical and current data.

Runs on both on-premise and cloud servers

Depending upon the specific need or to enhance flexibility in operation or to optimize cost, NebuLink analytic gives you complete freedom to run analytics on on-premise and cloud servers.

Development of custom analytic applications

NebuLink analytic empowers you to develop custom analytical applications based on the specific need of enterprise and scaling requirements to reduce maintenance costs or avoid equipment failures or improve business operations.

USE CASES

Smart Marketing

Based on data analysis of product usage and reviews NebuLink Analytics enables you to predict consumer behaviors, anticipate future Customer Needs, forecasting future purchases, and trends. It enables you to create new products and value-added services on outcome-based pricing.

Smart Manufacturing

By providing your real-time actionable data analysis, NebuLink Analytics not only provide predictive maintenance but also improve efficiency, reduce downtown, enhance transparency in production, ensure optimum resource utilization, reduced maintenance cost, and enhance the safety and security of equipment and workmen.

Smart Grid

With continuous processing of data in real-time, NebuLink Analytics enable smart grid operators to continuously monitor power generation and demand and use the optimum mix of conventional and non-conventional source of power generation to run the smart grid without any technical glitches.

Smart Health Care

By analyzing real-time data generated from sensors embedded in medical devices, diagnostic equipment, personal health equipment, etc NebuLink Analytics predicts and preempts the medical emergency thus improving healthcare outcomes and reducing health care costs.

Smart Security

Acting on real-time actionable data and video analysis NebuLink analytics enable city planners to protect the road accident, traffic jams, and crime. It provides greater insight for predicting future events and empowers city planners to formulate new policy or amend existing policy pertaining to the security of public places.

Smart Agriculture

NebuLink Analytics can automatically enrich smart agriculture devices data with other public data sources to provide your real-time actionable data from individual farms to use natural resources judiciously to enhance productivity and save the standing crops from weeds.

Smart Supply Chain Management

Whether it is essential commodities such as food or consumer durable or non-durable items, the Nebulae analytic enables you to provide real-time actionable data to constantly proactively replenish supplies from inventories to vending machines without any interruptions.
